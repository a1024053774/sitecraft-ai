What We Serve

禾巷小馆 is a neighborhood home-style restaurant serving nearby residents and office workers on their lunch break. The kitchen prepares ingredients for both lunch and dinner service, offering single-portion meal sets and hot dishes suited to sharing. We do not operate a bar or a banquet hall.

Dishes are served according to the day's preparation; sold-out items are marked on the in-store menu.

Dine-in and in-store takeout are available; we do not take on wedding banquets, private venue bookings, private chefs, or group catering.

Two Common Ways to Dine

Solo Lunch Break

How to OrderCome in during lunch, 11:00–14:00, and order from the items marked as available on the in-store menu that day. For one person, order the Braised Chicken Leg with Shiitake over Rice or the Lunch Single-Person Set. The kitchen stops taking lunch orders at 13:30.

What We Cannot GuaranteeWe make no commitment on minutes to serve, and we do not guarantee that all dishes are available on any given day.

Family Dinner to Share

How to OrderCome in during dinner, 17:00–20:30, and order the Tomato-Braised Beef Brisket and the Seasonal Vegetable and Tofu Casserole, with rice ordered separately, sharing several hot dishes together. The kitchen stops taking dinner orders at 20:00.

NoteThe sharing suggestion is not a fixed-size set meal and does not include drinks or extra side dishes.
